## Key Features

- **Studio-Grade Audio & Built-In DSP:** High-bitrate **48kHz Opus** streaming with a built-in **5-Band Equalizer**, **Sub-Bass Boost**, Virtualizer, and Loudness Enhancement across both audio and music video playback.
- **Seamless Audio & Full-Bleed Music Video (Up to 4K / 1080p):** Play standalone tracks or switch effortlessly into widescreen music videos with synchronized audio, quality selection (`144p` – `2160p`), and video captions.
- **Synchronized Multi-Source Lyrics:** Real-time synced lyrics fetched from YouTube Music timed cues, LRCLIB, and KuGou, with optional translation support.
- **Smart Radio & Discovery Queue:** Instant endless mixes, mood/genre exploration, and intelligent queue replenishment without duplicate tracks.
- **Offline Downloads & Local Library:** Save tracks offline, organize playlists, liked songs, albums, and artists, and back up/restore your library cleanly.

## Installation Notes

### Android (`Zentra-v2.7.0-stable.apk`)
1. Download `Zentra-v2.7.0-stable.apk` from [Releases](https://github.com/ahmadfajarf/zentra-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open the `.apk` file on your Android device (Android 9.0+).
3. If prompted, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser or file manager, then tap **Install**.

### Windows (`Zentra-Music-1.1.0-nsis.exe` / `Zentra-Music-1.1.0-portable.exe`)
- Choose **`Zentra-Music-1.1.0-nsis.exe`** for a standard Start Menu & desktop installation, or **`Zentra-Music-1.1.0-portable.exe`** to run directly with zero installation.
- **Windows SmartScreen Notice:** Because Zentra Music is an independent, self-published release without a commercial EV code-signing certificate, Windows Defender SmartScreen may display *"Windows protected your PC"* on first launch. Click **"More info"** -> **"Run anyway"** to launch safely.
